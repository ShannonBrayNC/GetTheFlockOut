## Content findings and evidence limits

The content generally distinguishes requests for policy change from proven misconduct; vendor claims from independent audits; historical counts from current inventories; and city, county, state and private operators. Preserve these distinctions in future edits.

- **Angier:** figures and quotations remain attributed to the supplied community response/analysis PDFs. The original police case log is not supplied, so case classifications, costs, image-volume estimates and quotation authenticity are not independently certified. Arrests/charges are not convictions. Current police leadership and elected names were checked against the town's published directories.
- **Erwin:** official elected/police pages corroborate the configured leadership. ABC11's reported counts and costs remain dated reporting, not a current inventory. A differing EFF count is disclosed rather than silently reconciled.
- **Fuquay-Varina:** citizen organizing, primary budget/minutes and an automated secondary transcript are identified separately. The site correctly rejects the claim that Flock never appeared in a budget. The transcript's counts, costs and contract chronology still require executed records/official recordings for independent confirmation.
- **Holly Springs:** the town's July 21 statement identifies Vigilant/Motorola, not a municipal Flock program. The petition correctly addresses routine ALPR collection and sharing. The official statement's historical claim about rotating equipment is not represented as today's complete inventory. External-agency installation authority remains an attributed report, not this site's legal conclusion.
- **Apex:** official council names and WRAL's October 1 review reporting corroborate the program/review context. An Axon vote is not a Flock renewal/cancellation. November 5's outcome and a future Flock hearing date remain unverified; the page tells readers to check the official agenda.
- **Selma:** the published government page lists Mayor McAllister, Scarboro, Whitley and Ray; the police page lists Chief Vause. The town describes four council seats but this government directory publishes only three councilmember contacts. Do not invent the missing address or treat this subset as a complete seat roster. Reddit allegations and spending claims remain attributed and unproven.
- **Wake Forest:** the town's 2023 announcement corroborates a 25-reader lease and historical default retention. Its current mayor/board page and June 2026 appointment announcement support the newer roster. Recommended budget lines are not proof of actual payment. A town meeting summary is explicitly distinguished from official minutes.
- **Wilmington:** the municipal roster is separate from county officials. September reporting supports no direct WPD Flock contract/access at that time while describing possible searches requested through other agencies. WECT's September 21 follow-up distinguishes the county's 20 leased ALPRs from eight Condor cameras. No city cancellation is asserted.
- **Statewide:** 4 confirmed terminations and 15 community-reported decisions are kept separate; 13 locations have reported citizen efforts, including New Hanover County in the confirmed category. The 15 reported decisions remain unverified, not confirmed cancellations. Camera removal, contract termination and bans are different claims. The tracker is not an exhaustive camera or agency inventory.
- **Law:** G.S. 20-183.32 supports the Angier page's general 90-day maximum with statutory preservation exceptions; it does not require retaining all records for 90 days. Captured plate data is confidential under that provision; requests for nonconfidential governance records are distinguished from requests for private plate histories.

## Remaining operational work and limits

1. **Inbox delivery is separate:** configured keys and provider acceptance are not proof an official received/read a message. This review did not send a live test, inspect a monitoring inbox or certify provider webhook configuration. Complete the documented monitoring-only test and authenticated webhook setup when needed.
2. **Real sign-in round trip:** the browser showed the ChatGPT sign-in entry point; no fresh authenticated production signature was submitted. Mocked route tests and live read-only readiness are the submission evidence from this review.
3. **Residency reviews remain manual:** check actual municipal residence and record the method before releasing authorized messages. Never approve based solely on postal city/ZIP. Keep signatures from unrelated petitions separate.
4. **Source follow-up remains necessary:** obtain current contracts, inventories, invoices and sharing/access records where the pages list open questions. Reported statewide decisions cannot be promoted without corroboration. Selma's unpublished fourth-seat contact needs town confirmation.
5. **Recovery:** backup encryption/readback fixtures passed. This review did not restore a live database or certify a production recovery. Test recovery into an isolated database with sending disabled.
6. **Readiness edge case:** `GET /api/petition` reports sender configuration readiness, but does not include the campaign-closed setting or an empty selected-recipient list in its readiness flag. POST independently rejects both. Currently all eight endpoints have nonempty recipient selections and the fixtures verify closed-campaign rejection; consolidate these indicators before relying on GET as a full campaign lifecycle status.

See each city's OPERATIONS.md for sender, webhook, approval and recovery procedures. Scheduled health checks must remain dry; this review did not release any queue or change automation schedules.
